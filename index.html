Who we are

Creating value beyond the farm gate.

Onivista Agro is a professionally managed food-processing company focused on premium fried onion, dehydrated onion and other value-added agricultural ingredients.

Our model connects farmers, processing, quality assurance, packaging and distribution to reduce post-harvest losses and build stronger market opportunities.
